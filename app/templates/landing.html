Frequently asked questions

What is LECFLOW?

LECFLOW (LectureHub) is a free lecture tracker for IIT Madras BS students. It syncs your public course calendars and brings live session links, recordings, and personal notes into one dashboard.

Do I need to make my personal calendar public?

No. LECFLOW only reads the public course calendar link published by IITM for each subject. Your personal Google Calendar is never accessed.

Does LECFLOW store the lecture videos?

No. Videos stay on the institute's Google Drive or YouTube. LECFLOW stores the links per lecture so you can find them instantly, even in later terms.

Can I take notes while attending a live session?

Yes. The notes editor sits next to the video with Markdown formatting, KaTeX math, code highlighting, autosave, and one-click PDF export.

Is LECFLOW affiliated with IIT Madras?

No. LECFLOW is an independent student-built tool and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by IIT Madras.
